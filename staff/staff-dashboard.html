Portal Workflow

Staff can prepare announcements and articles through the portal. When the backend is connected, submitted content can be sent to the administrator for approval before it becomes publicly visible on the school website.

Backend-ready: This dashboard is structured so authentication, permissions, student records, content submissions, approvals, notifications and other Staff Portal functions can later connect to secure backend endpoints.
