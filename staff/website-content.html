How It Works

Your uploaded content follows a review process before appearing publicly.

1️⃣
Submit Upload your photo or video and select the correct website section.
2️⃣
Administrator Review An administrator checks the submitted content.
3️⃣
Approval Approved content becomes eligible for publication.
4️⃣
Website Publication Approved content can then appear in the appropriate public website section.
