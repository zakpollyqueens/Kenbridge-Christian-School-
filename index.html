THE KENBRIDGE PROMISE

Caring, honest and hopeful.

Kenbridge Christian School exists to nurture learners through quality education, Christian values and a caring community.

We believe children need more than lessons. They need guidance, discipline, encouragement and opportunities to discover what they can become.

Our approach supports the whole learner — academically, spiritually, socially, physically and creatively.
