KENBRIDGE CHRISTIAN SCHOOL
WHO WE ARE

Education with purpose and values.

At Kenbridge Christian Schools we are a word-based, dynamic, non-denominational, life-transforming, Christian Institution.

Kenbridge was established to raise Word-Centered, skilled servant leaders with Christ-like character and send them out as agents of transformation and change.

Our education seeks to develop the whole person — Spirit, soul and body — while helping every learner discover God-given purpose and potential.

OUR JOURNEY

From humble beginnings to a growing legacy.

Since 2008, the Kenbridge journey has continued to develop while remaining rooted in Christian values.

2008
WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

A humble beginning

Kenbridge Christian Schools opened in 2008 as All Saints Junior School under Mr. Rwamakuba Ronald.

In its early days, the school operated with modest facilities and resources.

The school was established with the desire to raise Word-Centered, skilled servant leaders with Christ-like character who could become agents of transformation and change.

2020
A NEW CHAPTER

Continued development

In 2020, Mr. Tumusiime Ibrahim joined the school community, becoming part of an important chapter in the school's development.

The school profile records this period as part of the journey toward a new direction and vision for the institution.

2021
A NEW DIRECTION

Vision and transformation

In 2021, Mr. Rwamakuba Richard joined the school.

The school profile explains that a vision from God led to a massive shift and a new direction for the institution, strengthening its identity and purpose.

TODAY
WHERE WE ARE

Kenbridge today

Kenbridge continues to develop its learning environment, academic opportunities, spiritual formation and wider impact.

The school has also achieved milestones including UNEB certificates and an examination centre number, while continuing to participate in sports and other activities.

FUTURE
WHERE WE ARE GOING

Building the Kenbridge of tomorrow

The Kenbridge journey continues toward a wider national, regional, African and global impact.

The strategic direction includes Vision 7:2032, Vision 10:2042, Vision 10:2052 and Vision 20:2072, representing progressive aspirations for national, East African, African and global influence.

OUR DIRECTION

Vision, Mission & Strategy

The principles and direction that guide Kenbridge Christian Schools.

👁️

Our Vision

We are a global movement raising a new generation of Christ-centered leaders in all spheres of life.

Global movement: Kenbridge looks beyond one institution and seeks to contribute to transformation across communities and nations.

New generation: We seek to prepare learners who will carry responsibility and influence into the future.

Christ-centered leaders: Leadership is rooted in Christ, character, service and the Word of God.

Spheres of life: Learners are prepared to influence and serve in the different areas of life where God places them.

🎯

Our Mission

To raise word-Centered, academically sound, spirit-filled servant leaders with a Christ-like character who lead others to the Lord Jesus through a holistic education.

Word-centered: Learners are grounded in the Word of God.

Spirit-filled servant leaders: Learners are developed to serve, lead and influence others.

Christ-like character: Character formation is an essential part of the educational journey.

Holistic education: Education develops the whole person and equips learners for life and service.

🧭

Our Strategy

Kenbridge seeks to plant UNEB and Cambridge primary and secondary schools globally.

The strategy also includes discipling students in Missional Families, conducting annual evangelism outreaches through Missional Families and building the school's own Adullam Curriculum.

The Adullam Curriculum is intended to be taught in all schools alongside the main curriculum.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Every child has God-given potential.

Our philosophy begins with the uniqueness, purpose and potential of every learner.

🌱

Every Child Is Unique

Every child is unique, fearfully and wonderfully made by God and has a God-given purpose.

We believe every child has God-given potential. Education therefore recognises, nurtures and develops that potential.

Education develops the whole person — Spirit, soul and body.

We seek to lead learners into relationship with God, develop Christ-like character and servant leadership, and prepare them to understand their purpose, relevant skills, gifts and abilities.
