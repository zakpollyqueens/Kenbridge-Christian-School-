KENBRIDGE CHRISTIAN SCHOOL
WHO WE ARE

Education with purpose and values.

Kenbridge Christian School is a Christian-centered learning community committed to nurturing children through quality education, character development and strong moral values.

We seek to create an environment where every learner is encouraged to discover their abilities, develop confidence and prepare for a meaningful future.

Our approach recognizes the importance of developing the whole child — academically, spiritually, socially and physically.

OUR JOURNEY

From humble beginnings to a growing legacy.

Since 2008, Kenbridge Christian School has continued to grow, develop and create opportunities for learners while remaining rooted in Christian values.

2008
WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

A humble beginning

Kenbridge Christian School began operating in 2008 under the vision and leadership of its founder and director, Mr. Rwamakuba Ronald.

In its early days, the school was small and operated with modest facilities. The buildings were limited, resources were few, and the school did not yet have many of the facilities and resources it has today.

Despite these humble beginnings, the foundation of a school committed to education, character and Christian values had been established.

GROWTH
THE YEARS OF DEVELOPMENT

Growing beyond the beginning

With time, Kenbridge continued to develop its learning environment, facilities and opportunities for learners.

The school grew from its modest beginnings into a more established learning community, supported by teachers, parents, learners and school leadership.

Every stage of development became part of the continuing Kenbridge story.

TODAY
WHERE WE ARE

Kenbridge today

Today, Kenbridge Christian School has grown into a cleaner, more attractive and welcoming learning environment with improved school buildings and a welcoming reception.

The school's journey has also included achievements beyond the classroom, including football medals and cups and other sporting accomplishments.

Kenbridge has also reached important academic milestones, including receiving certificates from UNEB and obtaining an examination centre number.

FUTURE
WHERE WE ARE GOING

Building the Kenbridge of tomorrow

The journey of Kenbridge Christian School continues.

Looking ahead, the school aspires to continue improving its learning environment, strengthening academic excellence, expanding opportunities and helping every learner discover their potential.

The vision is to build upon the foundation established in 2008 while preparing today's learners for the opportunities and responsibilities of tomorrow.
