WHO WE ARE

Our Identity

At Kenbridge Christian Schools we are a word-based, dynamic, non-denominational, life-transforming, Christian Institution.

We are committed to raising a new generation of Christ-centered leaders who are equipped to influence and transform their spheres of life.

OUR STORY

Background & Journey

Kenbridge Christian Schools is a private, non-denominational Christian school established to raise Word-Centered, skilled servant leaders with Christ-like character and send them out as agents of transformation and change.

In 2008, the school opened as All Saints Junior School under Mr. Rwamakuba Ronald. In 2020, Mr. Tumusiime Ibrahim joined the school, followed by Mr. Rwamakuba Richard in 2021. A vision from God then led to a massive shift and a new direction for the institution.

OUR DIRECTION

Vision

We are a global movement raising a new generation of Christ-centered leaders in all spheres of life.

Global movement: A growing community of people and institutions committed to advancing God's Kingdom through education, discipleship and transformation.

New generation: Young people equipped to carry forward the values, faith, knowledge and responsibility needed to shape the future.

Christ-centered leaders: Leaders whose character, decisions and influence are rooted in Jesus Christ.

All spheres of life: Every area where people live, work, serve and influence society.

OUR PURPOSE

Mission

To raise word-Centered, academically sound, spirit-filled servant leaders with a Christ-like character who lead others to the Lord Jesus through a holistic education.

Word-Centered: Grounded in the Word of God.

Academically sound: Equipped with strong academic foundations.

Spirit-filled servant leaders: Learners who serve others and lead through the empowerment and guidance of the Holy Spirit.

Christ-like character: Demonstrating values and conduct that reflect the character of Christ.

Holistic education: Developing the learner in spirit, soul and body.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Our Philosophy

Every child is unique, fearfully and wonderfully made by God, and has a God-given purpose.

Every child has God-given potential. Education should recognise, nurture and develop that potential while developing the whole person — Spirit, soul and body.

We lead learners into a relationship with God, Christ-like character and servant leadership.

We prepare learners to understand their purpose, develop relevant skills, discover their gifts and abilities, serve God and others, and impact and advance God's Kingdom in their spheres of influence.
