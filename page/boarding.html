RESIDENTIAL LIFE

Helping learners grow together.

Boarding can provide learners with an environment where they can balance academic work, personal development, recreation and community life.

At Kenbridge Christian School, residential life is intended to complement the school's academic and Christian values.

Learners are encouraged to develop responsibility, respect, discipline and positive relationships with fellow members of the school community.
