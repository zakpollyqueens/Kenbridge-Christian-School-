RESIDENTIAL LIFE

Helping learners grow together.

Boarding can provide learners with an environment where they can balance academic work, personal development, recreation and community life.

At Kenbridge Christian School, residential life is intended to complement the school's academic and Christian values.

Learners are encouraged to develop responsibility, respect, discipline and positive relationships with fellow members of the school community.

Care, discipline and community Residential life is intended to support the learner's wider educational and personal development.

DAILY LIFE

A Balanced Routine

A structured routine can help learners balance academics, personal care, recreation and rest.

01
Morning preparation and personal care

Time for personal preparation before the school day begins.

02
School lessons and academic activities

Learners attend lessons and participate in the normal academic programme.

03
Meals and appropriate break periods

Regular meal and rest periods form part of a balanced daily routine.

04
Sports, recreation and co-curricular activities

Time can be provided for recreation, sports and other school activities.

05
Evening preparation, revision and study time

Learners can use appropriate study periods for homework and revision.

06
Rest and preparation for the next day

Adequate rest supports a healthy and balanced school routine.
