PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

Information about how this website may collect, use and protect information submitted by visitors.

01 — INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Information you provide

When you use forms on this website, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, telephone number and message.

The school may use information submitted through the website to respond to enquiries, feedback, admissions requests or other legitimate school-related communications.

Your information matters. Please provide only information that is necessary for the purpose of the enquiry or service you are requesting.
02 — HOW INFORMATION IS USED

Using submitted information

Information submitted through the website should only be used for purposes connected with the service or enquiry for which it was provided.

The school should take reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

03 — WEBSITE SECURITY

Protecting information

Website security measures may include secure hosting, access controls and appropriate protection of information submitted through online forms.

No internet transmission or online storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Visitors should therefore take reasonable care when submitting personal information online.

04 — THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

External services

The website may use external services such as mapping, analytics, hosting, communication or social-media services.

These services may have their own privacy policies and terms that apply when their features are used. Users should review the relevant policies of those services when appropriate.

05 — CHILDREN'S INFORMATION

Protecting learners

Because Kenbridge Christian School serves children, particular care should be taken when publishing or submitting information relating to learners.

Parents, guardians and authorized school personnel should ensure that information about learners is handled appropriately and only for legitimate school-related purposes.

Protecting learners is a shared responsibility. Photographs, names, academic information and other learner-related information should be handled in accordance with applicable school policies and requirements.
06 — YOUR QUESTIONS

Contact the school

If you have questions about information submitted through this website, please contact Kenbridge Christian School.
