HOW TO JOIN

Admission Process

A simple process to help your family get started.

01

Make an Enquiry

Contact the school to learn about available classes, requirements, fees and admission dates.

02

Submit an Application

Complete the admission application and provide the required information about the learner.

03

Assessment

Where applicable, the learner may be invited for an assessment or interaction with the school.

04

Admission Decision

The school communicates the admission outcome and provides information about the next steps.

05

Prepare for School
