JOIN KENBRIDGE

Welcome to our school community.

We welcome families who are looking for a supportive environment where children can grow academically, spiritually, socially and physically.

Our admissions process is designed to help parents and guardians understand our school, learning environment and expectations before joining.

For the most accurate admission requirements, available places and current school information, please contact the school directly.

HOW TO JOIN

Admission Process

A simple process to help your family get started.

01

Make an Enquiry

Contact the school to learn about available classes, requirements, fees and admission dates.

02

Submit an Application

Complete the admission application and provide the required information about the learner.

03

Assessment

Where applicable, the learner may be invited for an assessment or interaction with the school.

04

Admission Decision

The school communicates the admission outcome and provides information about the next steps.

05

Prepare for School

Parents receive the information needed to prepare the learner for the new school term.

06

Welcome to Kenbridge

Your child joins the Kenbridge Christian School community and begins their learning journey.
